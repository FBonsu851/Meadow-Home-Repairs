SYSTEM ADMINISTRATION

Admin

Add and remove people, reset passwords, arrange cover for the manager, check the security log and look after the system's data.

    People who can sign in

    Each person only sees the pages for their role. New people and password resets get a temporary password, shown once here. They must choose their own password the first time they sign in. Resetting, changing a role, disabling or removing someone signs them out straight away.

    Temporary password

    Give it to the person privately (in person or by phone). Do not send it in the same email as their sign-in name. It is not stored anywhere and cannot be shown again.

    Cover for the manager

    When the manager is away, a care worker or maintenance team member can be given the manager's access for a set time (up to 14 days). They keep their own pages too. Cover ends automatically at the time you choose, or you can end it early. Everything they do is recorded in the security log.

    Security log

    Sign-ins, wrong passwords, locked accounts, blocked pages and actions, account changes, cover and downloads. The latest 1,000 events are kept; download the log regularly to keep a longer record.

    System & data

    Download a backup regularly. Backups contain repairs, safety checks, certificates, announcements, settings and the security log, but never accounts or passwords. Photos and certificate files are stored separately on this device.

    Backup

    Save a copy of all records as a file.

    Restore

    Replace the current repairs, checks, announcements and settings with a backup file. Accounts are not changed.

    Sessions

    Sign everyone else out, for example after a security concern. They can sign straight back in.

    Training data

    Add six months of example repairs and checks for staff training, then remove them before going live. Real records are never changed.

    Web address

    The secure address where staff open the system, for example https://repairs.meadowhouse.org.uk/. It is printed in room QR codes and email alerts.

    Response targets

    Hours from a repair being reported to the team attending and to the work being completed.
