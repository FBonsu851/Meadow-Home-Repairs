      Safety checks & testing schedule

      Gas, electrical, fire, water, lifting and resident safety checks. Intervals follow common UK care home practice; adjust them to your own risk assessments and current regulations.

      Certificate register

      Gas Safety Record, EICR, LOLER and other certificates, with expiry dates. You and the maintenance team are alerted 30 days before a certificate expires. The full register is also in the inspection evidence pack (Overview & reports).

      Rooms & QR codes

      Print a QR code for each room and put it on the wall or door. Staff scan it with a phone or tablet camera, sign in, and the report form opens with the room already filled in. The room list also appears as suggestions on the report form.
